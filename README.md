Hospital Workflow Optimization & Centralized EMR Architecture 

★ Lead Business Analyst: Rohit Shorey  

★ Domain: Healthcare Operations & Systems Engineering  

---

📌 Executive Summary
An end-to-end Business Analysis case study evaluating operational bottlenecks across outpatient intake, physical file retrieval, and dispensary prescription hand-offs. The proposed solution establishes a centralized, cloud-hosted Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system integrated with digital self-service check-in and automated e-Prescribing workflows.

---

🎯 Target Impact & KPIs

• -40% Patient Wait Time: Reducing total cycle time from 90+ minutes to <35 minutes.

• 0% Prescription Errors: Full transition to digital physician order entry (e-Prescribing).

• <1.5s Record Retrieval: Sub-second patient medical history queries replacing manual 15–20 minute file room searches.

🔍 Core Methodologies Applied

• Stakeholder Analysis: Prioritization across executive sponsors, clinical end-users, IT security, and patients.

• Gap Analysis: Current manual state vs. cloud-relational future state.

• Requirements Engineering: Functional Requirements (FR-01 to FR-04) and Non-Functional Requirements (NFR-01 to NFR-03: AES-256 encryption, HIPAA compliance, 99.9% uptime SLA).
